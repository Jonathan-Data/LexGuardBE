# 🔮 Vision & Philosophy: Black Box to Glass Box

## The Transparency Gap
Traditional AI development often results in "Black Box" systems: models where inputs and outputs are known, but the internal decision-making process is opaque. As of August 2026, this is no longer legally viable for High-Risk AI systems in the European Union.

## The LexGuardBE Philosophy: "Glass Box" Governance
LexGuardBE enforces a **Glass Box** approach. This means:

1. **Explainability by Design**: Every decision cycle made by an AI system must be traceable back to its training data, logic branch, or RAG source.
2. **Deterministic Compliance Loops**: Using [[The Agentic Stack]], we wrap non-deterministic LLMs in deterministic state machines (LangGraph).
3. **Continuous Auditing**: Compliance is not a point-in-time check; it is a live state maintained throughout the software development lifecycle (SDLC).

## Bridging the Belgian Divide
Belgium's unique regulatory environment requires multi-lingual support (NL/FR/DE) and adherence to BIPT specific circulars. LexGuardBE is architected to prioritize these local nuances while maintaining a global EU standard.
