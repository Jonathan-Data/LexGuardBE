# 🇧🇪 Belgian BIPT AI Guidelines 2026

The **Belgian Institute for Postal services and Telecommunications (BIPT)** is the primary regulatory body for AI systems operating within the Belgian telecommunications and postal sectors, often setting the standard for other Belgian industries.

## Key Compliance Pillars
1. **Multilingualism**: All user-facing transparency disclosures must be available in Dutch, French, and German.
2. **Local Data Residency**: While the AI Act doesn't strictly mandate local hosting, the BIPT strongly recommends that High-Risk training data remains within the EEA, preferably with localized Belgian instances for critical infrastructure.
3. **Cybersecurity Circulars**: Alignment with NIS2 directives is mandatory for AI systems controlling essential services.
